# Style Reference Library

Use this only when style diagnosis, style capture, or no-sample improvement needs a mature visual reference. Pick 1 recommended direction and 1-2 alternatives.

## Leica-like / "German Look"

Fits: street, travel, quiet interiors, everyday scenes with strong light or geometry.

Look: dense midtones, firm blacks, restrained saturation, natural color, high microcontrast, clean geometry. The feeling is calm, weighted, precise.

Shoot: use real light, avoid messy backgrounds, look for strong edges and layered planes. Prefer 35mm/50mm equivalent or phone 1x/2x. Expose slightly low to protect highlights.

Edit: modest contrast, blacks -5 to -20, highlights -10 to -35, texture +5 to +20, clarity +3 to +12, vibrance -5 to +10. Avoid heavy teal/orange or over-smoothed skin.

## Japanese Airy / Clear Daily Life

Fits: windows, cafes, streets, plants, home scenes, soft daylight, quiet portraits.

Look: high brightness, low contrast, soft highlights, low saturation, gentle greens/blues, clean negative space.

Shoot: use window shade or cloudy light. Simplify the frame. Prefer phone 1x/2x or 35-50mm. Overly hard sun usually hurts this style.

Edit: exposure +0.2 to +0.8, contrast -5 to -20, highlights -10 to -30, shadows +10 to +35, blacks +5 to +20, greens saturation -10 to -30, blues luminance +5 to +20.

## Documentary / Pulitzer-like News Photograph

Fits: events, public spaces, human conflict, gesture, relationship, decisive moments.

Look: story first, beauty second. Strong subject relationship, readable context, real emotion, timing, layered information.

Shoot: move closer, wait for gesture, keep context, avoid decorative blur. Use 24-35mm or phone 1x for proximity and environment.

Edit: keep believable color and contrast. Avoid stylized presets. Use local exposure to clarify subject and preserve context.

## Vivian Maier-like Street Observation

Fits: street scenes, reflections, shadows, windows, strangers, self-reflection, urban geometry.

Look: observational, slightly detached, often square or centered, strong shadows/reflections, human presence inside urban structure.

Shoot: look for mirrors, glass, silhouettes, hats/coats/gestures, frame-within-frame. Try waist/chest height, 35-50mm, square crop.

Edit: black-and-white or muted color. Strong but not crushed blacks, highlight control, grain 10-25 when suitable.

## Cinematic

Fits: night streets, interiors, portraits, car/window light, strong directionality.

Look: controlled light ratio, foreground/background layers, lower saturation, color separation, protected highlights.

Shoot: choose one dominant light source. Add depth through foreground obstruction or background glow. Use 35-85mm or phone 2x/3x.

Edit: highlights -20 to -60, shadows -10 to +20 depending mood, clarity low/moderate, split warm highlights and cool shadows lightly. Avoid fake orange/teal extremes.

## Editorial Lifestyle

Fits: home, cafe, objects, food, clothing, family, everyday scenes needing polish.

Look: natural but arranged. Clear subject hierarchy, controlled clutter, soft directional light, warm but not yellow color.

Shoot: remove distractions, use window side light, choose 2-3 objects only, align edges. Phone 2x or 35-50mm often improves order.

Edit: white balance neutral-warm, highlights -10 to -30, shadows +5 to +25, texture +5 to +15 on objects, reduce yellow/green casts.

## Architectural / Memorial Landscape

Fits: monuments, buildings, bridges, plazas, water reflections, formal landscapes.

Look: symmetry, strong vertical/horizontal control, perspective, calm negative space, weather/time discipline.

Shoot: align center axis, level camera, use grid, wait for clean foreground. Prefer 24-35mm or phone 1x; use 2x for compression. Shoot sunrise/sunset/blue hour when possible.

Edit: transform first, crop excess sky if weak, highlights -20 to -50, texture +10 to +25 on structure, reduce distracting greens/blues, keep water/sky softer than the subject.
